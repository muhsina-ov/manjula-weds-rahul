# Royal Digital Wedding Invitation: Dr. Manjula & Dr. Rahul

A luxury South Indian & traditional Indian temple-themed digital wedding invitation web application for the wedding of **Dr. Manjula & Dr. Rahul** (23 - 25 November 2026, Terapanth Bhawan, Udhna, Surat).

## ✨ Features
- **Royal Envelope Opening**: Auspicious wax seal with Lord Ganesha motif (`॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥`) and unfolding entrance.
- **Temple Aesthetics**: Authentic stone temple backdrop, carved pillars, hanging brass deepams with flickering flame animation, and floral toran.
- **Traditional Invocations & Sacred Shlokas**: Sanskrit shlokas and blessings honoring ancestors and family lineage.
- **Live Wedding Countdown**: Real-time countdown to ceremonies on 23rd November 2026.
- **Ceremonies Schedule**:
  - 23 Nov: Mehendi Ceremony (1:00 PM onwards)
  - 24 Nov: Haldi Carnival (12:00 PM onwards) & Sangeet Gala Night (7:30 PM onwards)
  - 25 Nov: Baarat Aagman (1:00 PM), Sacred Phere (3:00 PM), Grand Reception (7:00 PM onwards), Vidaai (11:00 PM)
- **Add to Calendar (.ics)**: One-click export for Google, Apple, and Outlook calendars.
- **Venue & Google Maps**: Direct link to [Terapanth Bhawan, Udhna, Surat](https://share.google/MuknIQ9mJxqA9zH63) with one-click address copy.
- **RSVP & Direct Contact**: Quick Call & WhatsApp links for Chaudhary and Thakur family hosts.
- **Interactive Blessings Guestbook**: Guests can submit warm wishes with animated floral celebration.
- **Web Audio Ambient Music**: Built-in traditional Shehnai and Tanpura melody player.
- **Floating Petal Shower**: Canvas animation with marigold and rose petals.
